§ I — How to Use This Kit

These 10 variants are designed for A/B testing across Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram). Each variant targets a different psychological hook (problem, dream, social proof, authority, urgency, etc.) to identify what resonates best with South East Queensland homeowners.

A/B Test 3 at a time

Never launch all 10 at once. Rotate batches to find clear winners without diluting your budget.

Budget $50-$100/day each

Ensure each variant has enough daily spend to exit the learning phase and generate meaningful data.

Run 5-7 days minimum

Meta's algorithm requires time to optimize delivery. Do not pause ads prematurely before day 5.

Kill underperformers, scale winners

Pause ads with high CPL or low CTR. Shift the budget into scaling the best-performing variants.

Note: Each ad section includes Primary Text, Headline, Description, CTA recommendation, Audience target, and Image/asset pairing.

§ II — Audience Targeting

The following targeting structures define who will see your ads. Align the recommended audience from each ad variant to these exact parameters in Meta Ads Manager.

AUDIENCE 1 — COLD (Top of Funnel)

  • Location: Brisbane + Gold Coast + Sunshine Coast (25km radius around each city center)
  • Age: 35-65
  • Household income: Top 30%
  • Interests: Home renovation, Swimming pools, Outdoor furniture, Architectural Digest, House & Garden, Real estate
  • Behavior: Engaged shoppers, Premium brand affinity

AUDIENCE 2 — WARM (Retargeting)

  • Website visitors (last 30 days)
  • Video viewers (75%+ of the video ad)
  • Instagram engagement (likes, comments, saves)
  • Lookalike (1%) of existing customer list

AUDIENCE 3 — HOT

  • Lead magnet downloaders
  • Quote form abandoners
  • Email subscribers who haven't booked

§ V — Testing Methodology

Adopt a strict, data-driven approach. Meta Ads reward structured budgets and clear success parameters.

  1. Start with 3-5 variants in parallel.
  2. Run each for a minimum 5 days at $50-$100/day.
  3. Primary metric: Cost per Lead (target $80-$200 for high-ticket renovation).
  4. Secondary metric: Click-through rate (target 1.5%+).
  5. Kill ads below CTR 0.8% or CPL > $300.
  6. Scale winners by 20-30% daily until performance drops.
  7. Refresh creative every 4-6 weeks to avoid ad fatigue.

§ VI — Compliance Notes

Ensure these rules are followed strictly to avoid account bans or legal complications.

  • Disclose business name in ads (YBMT Pool Decks / Your Building & Maintenance Team).
  • Include QBCC license number if making compliance claims.
  • Avoid making specific cost guarantees in ad copy.
  • Use Meta's targeting rules — no direct income/employment targeting (use proxies like Premium Brand Affinity).
  • Include physical business address in landing page footer (Meta requirement for transparency).
