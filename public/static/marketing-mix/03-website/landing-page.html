Why Most Pool Decks Fail Within 5 Years

We've built our entire business around fixing what generalists get wrong.

Bad Materials

Generalist builders spec materials that simply cannot handle QLD pool chemicals, intense UV exposure, and constant humidity.

Compliance Traps

Decks that don't meet the strict QLD Pool Safety Code MP 3.4 guidelines trigger expensive tear-downs and mandatory retrofits.

Poor Drainage

Water pooling under decks rots the substructure within years. Proper concealed drainage is critical for longevity.

Questions Our Clients Ask

How much does a pool deck cost in SEQ?
Most projects fall between $50,000 and $120,000 depending on size, materials, and complexity. Smaller projects start from $25,000. Your free consultation includes an exact quote tailored to your home.
How long does a pool deck take to build?
A typical pool deck takes 10-21 days to build, with design and approvals adding another 2-4 weeks beforehand. We provide a detailed timeline with every quote.
Do you handle council approvals and pool fence compliance?
Yes — completely. Council approvals, engineering certifications, pool fencing compliance under QDC MP 3.4, and the final Pool Safety Certificate are all handled by us. You don't lift a finger on paperwork.
What's the best material for a pool deck in QLD?
There's no single right answer — it depends on your home, lifestyle, and budget. We work with composite, spotted gum, merbau, and travertine. Your consultation includes guidance on which is right for you.
Can you build a deck around an existing pool?
Absolutely — that's about 70% of our work. We design new decks that integrate seamlessly with existing pool structures and fencing.
Do you build decks before a pool is installed?
Yes — and pre-pool deck planning is one of the smartest things you can do. We coordinate with your pool builder to ensure substructure, drainage, and compliance are built in from day one.
What warranty do you offer?
Every project comes with a written 7-year workmanship warranty. Material warranties (up to 25 years for composite) are passed directly to you.
What areas do you service?
All of South East Queensland — Brisbane (and surrounds), the Gold Coast, and the Sunshine Coast. From Noosa to Coolangatta and everywhere in between.
