B. Trigger Configuration

Ensure the nurture sequence triggers immediately upon either of the following events. Note: Both should add the lead to the exact same sequence to avoid duplication.

  • Trigger 1: Form Submit (Buyer's Guide PDF download)
  • Trigger 2: Form Submit (Free quote request form)

C. Personalization Tokens

Token Name Usage / Fallback
[First Name] Required in all emails. Fallback: "there" (e.g., "Hi there,")
[Region] Capture if possible: Brisbane / Gold Coast / Sunshine Coast
[Lead Source] PDF Guide or Quote Form
[Timeline] Capture timeframe (Now, 3-6mo, 12mo+)

D. Tags to Apply

List the tags every lead should receive upon entering the sequence:

  • Lead-Source-PDF or Lead-Source-Quote-Form
  • Region-Brisbane / Region-Gold-Coast / Region-Sunshine-Coast
  • Timeline-Now / Timeline-3-6mo / Timeline-6-12mo
  • Nurture-Active

E. Branching Logic

Goal Achieved: If lead books consultation at any point → Remove from nurture sequence immediately + Add to Consultation-Booked tag.
Gentle Decline: If lead replies "not now" → Pause sequence + Add to Long-Term-Nurture tag (schedule 90-day check-in).

F. Reply Handling Protocol

CRITICAL: All replies must route to a real human within 2 business hours during operating times. Set up the reply-to field as a monitored inbox (e.g., [email protected]). Do not use a no-reply address.

G. Tracking Metrics

  • Open Rates: Target 35%+
  • Click-Through Rates (CTR): Target 5%+
  • Consultation Bookings: Target 8-15 booked per 100 leads

H. Spam Avoidance Tips

  • Avoid ALL CAPS subject lines.
  • Avoid spammy words ('FREE', 'GUARANTEED', '$$$') repeated excessively.
  • Always include a physical business address in the email footer.
  • Use a real sender name (e.g., "Gerry at YBMT") rather than generic aliases.
  • Maintain a healthy text-to-image ratio (don't rely entirely on image-heavy emails).
  • Authenticate your domain fully with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
